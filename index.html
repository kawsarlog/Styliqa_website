The studio

Precision is the whole craft.

Styliqa is a fashion design & product-development studio founded in 2019 in Bangladesh, working with apparel brands, emerging designers and Etsy pattern sellers across the US, UK, Europe, the Middle East and beyond.

We handle menswear, womenswear, childrenswear, activewear, streetwear, swimwear, formalwear and workwear — guiding every piece from a rough idea to a production-ready file. Every pattern is drafted, graded across sizes, and fit-tested before it ever reaches a factory floor, because a five-minute grading mistake becomes a five-thousand-unit problem.

How a project runs

Five stages. One production-ready file.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Concept

    Send your sketch, reference or idea — plus fabric and measurements if you have them. We scope the project and timeline together.

  2. 02

    Pattern Making

    A precise base pattern is drafted to capture your design intent and fit.

  3. 03

    Grading

    The pattern is graded across your full size range, keeping proportion and fit consistent from size to size.

  4. 04

    Tech Pack & 3D Fit Check

    Full documentation — POM sheet, BOM, trims, labels — plus a 3D visualization to catch fit issues before sampling.

  5. 05

    Production-Ready Delivery

    Source files in AI, PDF, JPEG, PNG or EPS — with unlimited revisions until it's right.
