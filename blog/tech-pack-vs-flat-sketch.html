Tech Pack vs. Flat Sketch: What Your Factory Actually Needs

Styliqa StudioJuly 15, 20264 min read

A flat sketch shows the design. A tech pack tells the factory how to make it — measurements, construction, materials, and finish — so you are not trading five emails for every stitch.

If you are briefing production, start with the pack. Use the sketch as the visual layer inside it, not as a stand-in for the document.
