# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fashion brand founders, emerging designers, and Etsy sewing-pattern sellers — mostly outside Bangladesh (US, UK, Europe, Middle East, Australia), discovering Styliqa via Fiverr or Instagram. They land on the site while vetting a technical partner: they need someone who can turn a sketch or idea into a production-ready tech pack, graded pattern set, or 3D-visualized sample without costly factory mistakes. They are often mid-consideration, comparing a few freelancers/studios, and want proof of technical competence (real work, real reviews) before messaging.

## Product Purpose

A single-page brand/portfolio site for Styliqa, a fashion design & product-development studio (tech packs, pattern making & grading, 3D garment visualization, PDF sewing patterns for Etsy sellers). It exists to convert a cold visitor into an inquiry (Contact/DM/booking) by proving technical credibility and taste in the same breath — success is a visitor understanding within seconds "this is a fashion technical studio," trusting the craft, and reaching out.

## Brand Personality

Blend of boutique elegance and technical precision — Styliqa sits between a couture atelier and a garment factory's technical office. Confident, precise, warm rather than cold-corporate. Voice: capable, calm, detail-obsessed ("we get the pattern right so the factory doesn't get it wrong"), not hypey or salesy. Visual anchor: the existing red-circle hanger logo (deep maroon/burgundy) and a warm ivory/paper backdrop — read as refined fashion studio, not SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references

No strong client preference stated; default to general AI-slop avoidance — no generic Canva-template grids, no cold corporate/SaaS gradients or hero-metric-dashboard blocks, no stock-photo-of-models filler, no uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, no side-stripe cards, no gradient text.

## Design Principles

- Prove technical competence and taste simultaneously — pattern-grading precision (grids, technical line-drawing motifs) paired with editorial warmth (serif headlines, generous whitespace), never one without the other.
- Every section earns its place toward one outcome: an inquiry. Portfolio and credibility (reviews, stats, process) run in service of the contact action, not as a separate goal.
- Show, don't claim — real numbers (4.8★, 1400+ reviews, since 2019), real client language, real service scope, instead of generic "creative fashion designer" copy.
- Motion should feel like precise tailoring, not decoration: smooth, exponential ease-outs, purposeful reveals — never bouncy or gimmicky.
- Warmth lives in typography, color, and voice — not in a default cream body background applied reflexively.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/heading text ≥3:1. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (crossfade/instant, no gating content visibility on scroll-triggered animation). Keyboard-navigable nav and form. No color-only signaling (e.g. star ratings paired with numeric text).
